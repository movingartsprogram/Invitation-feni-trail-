The Feni Trail

With a full heart, we invite you

Dear friend,

Before The Feni Trail opens its doors to the public, we wanted it to be met first by the people who matter most to us.

So we are inviting a very few, carefully chosen and deeply trusted friends, and you are one of them. It is no small thing to hand someone a dream that is still being written. We do it because we trust you, and because your eyes and your honesty are what will make it worthy.

Come and spend a day and a night with us at Dudhsagar Plantation, the family farm where Ouro has been made by the same hands for generations. Come as our guest, and take in the farm as we know it: hills and quiet, birdsong at dusk, green in every direction, and a natural swimming pool to slow the whole day down.

We will see you at lunch, and we will send you off after breakfast.

What awaits you

A day and a night on the farm

The Natural Swimming Pool

Cool, clear water in the middle of the green, for a long, lazy swim.

The Water Trail

Walk in the stream itself, all the way to the little river where the trail ends.

The Scorpio Trail

Real scorpions, in the wild, shown by those who know where to look.

The Spice Trail

A slow walk through the plantation, past black pepper vines, banana palms and the cashew and coconut groves. Crush a leaf and smell the spice where it grows. It is the same farm that goes into Ouro, our spiced cashew feni.

The Copper Still

It is not cashew season, so our still will run on water this time. Everything else stays the same: the wood fire, the copper, every step of the process, and you will see exactly how Ouro is made. A very pure rehearsal, if a rather mild one.

Elvis Lobo, under the open sky

Goa's own guitar legend, playing just for us.

A barbecue under the stars

Good food, feni cocktails, and a long unhurried night.

Moon Spotting

When everything ends, we step out under the open sky to spot the moon. One quiet last look before we turn in.

All we ask in return is that you be yourself. Tell us what moved you, and tell us what we could do better. There is no greater gift you could give us at the very beginning.

From the bottom of our hearts, thank you for being here with us.
